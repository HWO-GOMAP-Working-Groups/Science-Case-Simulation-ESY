## 3. Presentation: Challenges in Computing Eta Earth

**Presenter:** Steve Bryson
- **Key Topics Covered:**
  - Historical spread of Eta Earth values and the challenges in defining and computing it.
  - Importance of reliability and completeness in planet catalogs, especially in the context of the Kepler data.
  - Impact of detection limits, instrumental artifacts, and small sample sizes on Eta Earth calculations.
  - Emphasis on the need for more accurate vetting of planet candidates and better understanding of planet populations.
  - Discussion on future improvements in Eta Earth estimation, including a five-year project aimed at reprocessing Kepler data with enhanced methodologies.

## 4. Q&A Session

- **Kevin W. Fogarty** asked about the small uncertainties in some studies of Eta Earth. Steve Bryson explained that these studies often extrapolate from regions with many planets, leading to lower uncertainties, but potentially less accurate results.
- **Ruslan Belikov** inquired about the use of Eta Earth assumptions in mission concepts for habitable worlds. Steve Bryson mentioned ongoing efforts and hinted at new best-effort calculations to be published soon.
- **Chris Stark** questioned the potential reduction in Eta Earth uncertainties after the five-year project. Steve Bryson cautiously estimated that uncertainties could be reduced by half.
